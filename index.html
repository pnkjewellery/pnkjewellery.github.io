Our heritage

Over five generations of goldsmiths. Not traders — makers.

Most Jewellery sell gold. Our family has shaped it for more than five generations, at the same kind of anvil you see here. The craft was never bought or outsourced; it was handed down, generation to generation, piece by piece.

“The hammer in our workshop has passed through more than five generations of hands — every one of them family.
